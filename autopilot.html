How this window works

This window is Autopilot's. It applies here to the applications Kaidostar gives it - ones you (or Auto, after your undo window) approved, on employers' hiring systems, handed to it since you turned it on - one at a time, filling each from your profile, your resume and your answer bank. It submits only when every rule passes; anything that needs you (a question your answer bank doesn't cover, a CAPTCHA, a site check or sign-in, a company's own site) is left on the Auto page with the reason, and its tab closes. One it submitted but couldn't confirm stays here for you to check.

Click or type in the tab it's applying in and that application is yours: Autopilot won't submit it on its own, and it stops for now so it never gets in your way. Using a tab it kept for you stops it after the one it's on. Close this window to stop this run; turn Autopilot off in the extension's popup to stop it altogether.
