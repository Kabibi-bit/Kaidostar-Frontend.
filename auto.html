When enabled, every scan automatically drafts and queues applications, and drafts referral outreach emails for matches worth reaching out to - no starring or clicking through cards required.

Before you turn this on

Auto drafts everything unattended - applications and outreach emails both - and queues it all in the Workshop. Nothing gets sent without you: every application still needs approval or clears your threshold with a 30-minute undo window, and every outreach email sits as a draft until you explicitly click Send (or "Send all pending") in the Workshop. Review the Workshop regularly while this is on.

Enable Auto

Every scan cycle, Kaidostar drafts a tailored application and a referral outreach email for each eligible match, auto-approving applications that clear your threshold below. Everything lands in the Workshop for review.

This is the real match score itself - roadmap alignment is already factored in as a graded part of that score, not a separate bonus on top. 97% is the real ceiling; no match ever scores higher. Lower the threshold to auto-approve more matches; raise it to keep more under manual review.
