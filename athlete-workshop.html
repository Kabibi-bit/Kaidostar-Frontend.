What this actually does

This does not edit, merge, or export video - that would need either heavy video processing in your browser or real server infrastructure, neither of which exists here. What it does for real: plays your own local video file entirely in your browser (the file never leaves your computer), lets you mark exact in/out timestamps, tag each clip to a segment from your Content Coach reel structure, and export a real, usable shot list to hand to any actual video editor (iMovie, CapCut, Premiere, etc.).
