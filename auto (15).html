Before you turn this on

Auto drafts everything unattended and queues it in the Workshop. By default nothing gets sent without you: applications either wait for approval or clear your threshold with a 30-minute undo window, and outreach emails sit as drafts until you click Send. Turn on autonomous apply below and that changes - matches that pass your rules get submitted on the next scan with no undo window, so you never have to open the app. Your rules decide what Auto even touches, so you can keep it narrow and trusted. Review the Workshop regularly while this is on.

Autonomous apply

Trust the engine to act for you. With this on, any match that passes your rules below and clears your confidence threshold is applied to automatically - submitted without waiting for your review first. You still set the rules and the daily cap, every application is logged below, and you can switch this off in one click. It's off by default: applications wait for your approval until you allow this.
