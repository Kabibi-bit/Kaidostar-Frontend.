Auto

Applies to the right jobs while you’re away

Auto picks only the jobs that clear your bar, prepares each application with your own resume and your own answers, and sends it - or hands you a ready kit when a site doesn’t allow automation. Fewer, better applications, every one with a reason and a record.

Auto never submits on LinkedIn, Indeed or other job boards that forbid automation, never guesses an answer, and keeps a record of everything it sends.
